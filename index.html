My Dearest, Aca

Ca, I’m sorry I didn’t use Mati Suri di Taman by Sore. I chose Liz on Top of the World instead because I know how much you love it, and I wanted to use something that reminds me of you. Maybe it’s a little cheesy, but I like knowing that whenever you hear it, there’s a little piece of me attached to something you already love.

You’re always writing things for me, editing me into your little texts, turning random moments into something worth keeping. You somehow always know how to put me into words better than I ever could myself. You can be my writer, and I’ll be your inspiration, but I don’t want to be just another thing you write about. I want to be part of your whole story. Your favorite chapter, your best line, the person you keep coming back to no matter how the story goes.

And if you’re Liz on top of the world, then I’ll be right there beside you. I’ll be your Darcy when you need one, your favorite person to come home to, and maybe even the reason you keep writing a little longer. Let me be somewhere in every page, even if it’s just a small sentence you never want to erase.

You’ve given me so many words, so many little things to keep, and more reasons to smile than you probably realize. So this time, let me give you something back. I want you to know that I’m ridiculously, hopelessly, and completely in love with you. And if I had to choose where I’d want to be in this whole damn world, I’d choose right beside you, on top of the world, watching the person I love most exist exactly as he is.

So keep writing, Chase. I’ll keep giving you reasons to write.

Simon, ♥
